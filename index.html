About Me

I am currently a Postmaster Researcher at the University of Messina, Italy, where my research focuses on developing deep learning models for movement intention detection using surface electromyography (sEMG) signals. My work aims to advance intelligent human–machine interaction systems, particularly in rehabilitation robotics and assistive technologies.

Previously, I served as a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Adama Science and Technology University for two years, and as a Lecturer in the Faculty of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Arba Minch University for five years. Through these roles, I gained extensive experience in teaching, curriculum development, and academic research.

I earned my Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Engineering from Addis Ababa University in 2022, and my Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Electrical and Computer Engineering, with a specialization in Computer Engineering, from Arba Minch University in 2017.
