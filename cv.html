Professional Summary

I am a dedicated researcher at the University of Messina, Italy, specializing in the development of advanced machine learning models for controlling upper limb movements through surface electromyographic signals.

I hold a Master of Science in Computer Engineering from Addis Ababa University, and my undergraduate degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering, with a focus on Computer Engineering, was earned at Arba Minch University in Ethiopia.

Currently, I am working on a cutting-edge research grant titled "Development of Machine Learning Models for Controlling Upper Limb Movements Using Surface Electromyographic Signals." This project blends my deep passion for both machine learning and biomedical engineering to create solutions with real-world implications for rehabilitation.

With a strong academic background and a deep commitment to advancing the fields of computer science and engineering, I am constantly striving to push the boundaries of innovation and contribute to meaningful technological advancements.
