Professional Summary

I'm a lecturer at Adama Science and Technology University and a Postmaster Researcher at the University of Messina, Italy. I received my Master of Science in Computer Engineering from Addis Ababa University and my undergraduate degree at Arba Minch University in Electrical and Computer Engineering with concentration in Computer Engineering.

I am currently a lecturer at the School of Electrical Engineering and Computing where I continue to teach and do research in the field of Computer Science and Engineering.
